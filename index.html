KPI Metrics — Explained

How we'll know the raffle is actually working — plain version, for review before George's approval

Why not just compare raffle vs. raffle?

Because normal growth already happens without a raffle. If we added 50 loyalty members a month before the raffle, and we're still adding 50 a month with the raffle — the raffle changed nothing. We measure the raffle against what would've happened anyway, not against last cycle.

50 / mo

vs

60 / mo

What we track — in priority order

PRIMARY — Loyalty
  • New loyalty sign-ups
  • Loyalty redemptions
  • Points spent entering the raffle
  • Unique loyalty participants
SECONDARY — Instagram
  • Net follower growth
  • Follower growth lift %
PARTICIPATION
  • Total raffle entries
  • Eligible entries
  • # of raffle cycles that month

The two numbers that matter

LIFT
Actual − Baseline
e.g. 60 − 50 = +10
LIFT %
Lift ÷ Baseline
e.g. 10 ÷ 50 = +20%
